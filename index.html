Why This Matters

A technology decision can look sound while important questions about the underlying problem, the way work is actually performed, information quality, organizational tradeoffs, evidence, expected benefits, and total investment are still unresolved. Finding those issues before a major commitment preserves options and can prevent expensive rework later.
