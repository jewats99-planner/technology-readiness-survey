Before you commit to the technology, can you answer these three questions?

This quick check takes about a minute. It is designed to expose uncertainty before a technology decision becomes expensive to reverse.

No registration. No email. No company information. No sales pitch.

1. Are we certain we understand the problem?
Can we clearly explain what is not working, who it affects, and what evidence shows the problem is significant?
2. Have we heard from everyone the change will affect?
Do our requirements reflect the needs of the organization—not just the department sponsoring the project?
3. Do we understand what making the change will really require?
Do we understand the people, time, process changes, assumptions, internal effort and total investment—not just the technology purchase?
Please answer all three questions.
